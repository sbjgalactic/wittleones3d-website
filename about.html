About Us

At Wittle Ones 3D, every critter and habitat starts as a digital design and ends up as a tiny treasure made by hand. We print each piece to order in colourful PLA, then finish and pack it ourselves, so every Wittle One gets a little extra care.

From pocket-sized minis and fidgets to playsets full of cosy details, we make things that are fun to collect, play with and give as gifts. We’re a small Australian business, and we love seeing our creations find new homes.

How a Wittle One is made

  1. 1

    Designed

    Every piece starts life as a digital design, ready to be brought into the real world.

  2. 2

    Printed

    When you order, we print your Wittle One layer by layer in bright, colourful PLA.

  3. 3

    Finished

    Each print is checked and tidied up by hand so it’s ready to play with and display.

  4. 4

    Packed

    We pack every order ourselves and send it off to its new home with a little extra care.

What we’re about

  • Made to order Nothing sits on a shelf. Your piece is printed just for you.
  • Made by hand Printed, finished and packed by us, not a warehouse.
  • Made in Australia A small local business that loves what it makes.
