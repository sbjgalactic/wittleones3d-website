About Us

At Wittle Ones 3D, every critter and habitat starts as a digital design and ends up as a tiny treasure made by hand. We print each piece to order in colourful PLA, then finish and pack it ourselves, so every Wittle One gets a little extra care. From pocket-sized minis and fidgets to playsets full of cosy details, we make things that are fun to collect, play with and give as gifts. We’re a small Australian business, and we love seeing our creations find new homes..
